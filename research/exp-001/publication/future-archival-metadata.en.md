# Future Zenodo and ORCID metadata plan

This plan prepares metadata only. No Zenodo deposit, DOI reservation, ORCID modification, or external account action is authorized by PUB-R2.

## Future Zenodo release

A separately authorized deposit should contain both EN and PT-BR EXP001-P0 documents, identify each language variant, release version, publication date, corresponding Git commit/tree, complete artifact hashes, license/attribution, scientific limitations, and the issued DOI. The deposit must preserve the equality of scientific authority between languages and must not represent documentary protocol validation as experimental results.

## Future ORCID association

After a DOI exists and under separate authority, bilingual metadata may be used to associate the research output with the researcher's ORCID record. ORCID should be described as a persistent researcher/output identifier service; it does not store or publish the complete protocol documents.

```text
ZENODO_DEPOSIT=NOT_AUTHORIZED
ORCID_MODIFICATION=NOT_AUTHORIZED
NASA_CONTACT=NOT_AUTHORIZED
```
